I am a graduate student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC). My research interests include robotics, human-robot interaction, natural language processing, and synthetic biology, with a particular interest in autonomous systems for scientific discovery and space exploration.

I currently conduct research in [Dr. Cynthia Matuszek's Interactive Robotics and Language (IRAL) Lab](https://iral.cs.umbc.edu/), where I explore how natural language, human gestures, and environmental context can help robots resolve ambiguous instructions in multi-robot settings, particularly in dynamic and hazardous environments. I am also a member of [Dr. David C. Garcia's Cell-Free Synthetic Biology Lab](https://david-c-garcia.github.io/), where my interests include computational biological engineering and laboratory automation.

Previously, I interned at [NASA's Johnson Space Center](https://www.nasa.gov/johnson/), supporting activities related to International Space Station payloads, technology demonstrations, and automation systems.
